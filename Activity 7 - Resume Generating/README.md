# Activity 7 – AI Tools: AI-Generated Nursing Resume

## Overview
This activity used ChatGPT and Claude AI to help create a professional resume for a simulated application as a newly licensed Registered Nurse (RN) applying for a Nurse I position in the Renal Dialysis Unit (RDU). The outputs were compared based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality.

## Objective
To explore how different AI tools can assist in preparing a professional resume, compare their outputs, and evaluate the importance of reviewing AI-generated content for accuracy and suitability.

## Task and Prompt
The prompt requested a modern, professional resume using the existing resume as the primary reference. It specified a clean healthcare-oriented design, preferably one page and no more than two pages, with a white background and navy blue and muted teal accents.

The resume was to emphasize patient-centered nursing care, assessment and monitoring, safe medication administration, infection prevention and control, documentation, communication, teamwork, adaptability, and willingness to learn renal nursing skills.

**Important accuracy requirement:** The prompt explicitly stated that the applicant had no actual hemodialysis or Renal Dialysis Unit clinical experience. The resume therefore needed to highlight transferable nursing competencies without inventing dialysis experience, certifications, or employment.

## AI Tools Used
- ChatGPT
- Claude AI

## Required Resume Sections
The prompt provided information for the following sections:
- Professional Profile
- Educational Background
- Clinical Experience presented as supervised Related Learning Experiences (RLE), not employment
- Core Clinical Competencies
- Seminars and Training
- Leadership and Organizational Experience
- Achievements and Recognition
- References (Available upon request)

## Comparison of AI Outputs

### ChatGPT
The documented ChatGPT output provided a straightforward text-based foundation with professional wording. However, the submitted comparison describes it as incomplete and lacking the visual layout, alignment, and full organization needed for a submission-ready resume.

### Claude AI
The documented Claude AI output presented a more complete and organized resume. It used navy blue and teal accents, consistent typography, organized spacing, and clearly separated sections. The comparison identified it as the more polished and visually appealing output.

### Overall Evaluation
According to the comparison in the submitted activity, Claude AI performed better in structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality. ChatGPT provided a useful textual foundation, while Claude AI produced the more complete visual resume.

## Final Output
The submitted documentation identifies the Claude AI resume as the final output. It presents Juliam L. Alderite, RN as a newly licensed RN and PNLE passer applying for a Nurse I position in the Renal Dialysis Unit. The resume highlights supervised RLE exposure and transferable clinical competencies without claiming actual dialysis experience.

## Reflection
This activity helped me understand how different AI tools can produce different results even when given similar instructions. ChatGPT was helpful in developing and organizing the resume content, while Claude AI provided a more polished and visually appealing layout. Comparing both outputs allowed me to recognize the importance of structure, clarity, and formatting in creating a professional CV. I also learned that AI-generated outputs still require human evaluation to ensure accuracy and suitability for the intended purpose. Overall, this activity improved my understanding of how AI tools can assist in preparing professional documents.
