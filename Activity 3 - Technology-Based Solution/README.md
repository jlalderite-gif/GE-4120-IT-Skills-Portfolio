# Activity 3 – Technology-Based Solution

## Project Title

**HOMEHELP PH: A Technology-Based Support and Referral Platform for Homeless Children and Families**

## Introduction

Homelessness of children and families is a serious social issue in the Philippines. Homeless children may experience poverty, disrupted education, inadequate housing, abuse, and exploitation. Accessing appropriate assistance and services can also be challenging.

This project proposes a technology-based solution that can make information and existing support services more accessible to people who need them.

## Proposed Solution

**HOMEHELP PH** is a proposed mobile and web application that connects homeless individuals, families, and vulnerable children to organizations and agencies that can provide assistance.

The application could provide:

- Nearby shelters and social service centers
- Food assistance
- Health services
- Educational assistance
- Financial assistance
- Online referral services
- Emergency contact information
- Donation and service opportunities

The platform could connect users with agencies such as the **Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD)** and local social welfare offices. It could also be integrated with the DSWD **Pag-Abot Program**, which provides outreach, referrals, shelter, and other services to people staying on the streets.

## How HOMEHELP PH Can Help

HOMEHELP PH can help vulnerable individuals and families by making available services easier to find and access.

The platform can help users:

1. Find nearby shelters and support centers.
2. Access information about available social services.
3. Connect with social workers and government agencies.
4. Request referrals for appropriate assistance.
5. Find emergency contacts.
6. Connect organizations and donors with communities in need.

## Expected Impact

HOMEHELP PH may improve accessibility to existing support services for homeless children and families. It can help connect people in need with shelters, social workers, healthcare, education, financial assistance, and other available resources.

Technology alone cannot solve homelessness. However, it can improve communication between vulnerable communities and the authorities and organizations that can provide assistance.
